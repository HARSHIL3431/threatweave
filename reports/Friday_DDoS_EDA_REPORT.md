## 5. Cybersecurity & Network Behavioral Findings
### A. Destination Port Targeting
- **99.998% of DDoS flows (128,024 / 128,027)** targeted **Port 80** (HTTP Web Server).
- 3 outlier attack flows connected to dynamic ports (64869, 64873, 27636).
- Benign traffic targeted Port 53 (DNS, 48,154 flows), Port 443 (HTTPS, 30,551 flows), and Port 80 (HTTP, 14,028 flows).

### B. DDoS vs DoS Behavioral Comparison
- **DDoS (Friday)** vs **DoS Hulk (Wednesday)**:
  - While both flood Port 80, the Friday DDoS was generated by coordinated LOIC agents across multiple client IPs, generating distinct source ports and maintaining a low duplicate rate (1.17% vs Wednesday's 11.82%).
  - Flows show moderate request payload lengths with short connection lifespans and rapid TCP teardowns.
